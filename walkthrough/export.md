# Save SVG or PNG

Click "Export" in the toolbar:

- Scale: 1x, 2x or 4x.
- "Max width" and "Max height": optional limits in pixels. The PNG shrinks to fit them and keeps its shape. The menu shows the final size before you save.
- "Save PNG" and "Save SVG" open a save dialog next to the source file.
- "Copy PNG" puts the image on the clipboard, ready to paste into a chat, a slide or an issue.
- "Copy SVG code" copies the SVG as text.

Exports use the background you picked in the toolbar: the editor color, white, transparent or a custom color.
